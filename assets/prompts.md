# Генерация спрайтов Луны

Инструмент: встроенный ImageGen. Первый лист сгенерирован по фото из запроса, второй — по первому листу.

## Основные позы

Use case: stylized-concept. Input 1 is identity and clothing reference. Generate a polished pixel-art game animation sheet for Luna, a compact chibi adult gothic elf based on the photo: silver-lilac twin ponytails with large black bows, long elf ears, sage-green eyes, black ring choker and cross earring, oversized charcoal sweater with small embroidered butterfly, black boots. Clearly readable small, 32-bit handheld game pixel art, crisp deliberate pixel clusters, limited charcoal/lavender/green palette, fine facial expression, full body in every cell. All sprites face RIGHT in a consistent 3/4 view. Exactly 4 columns by 4 rows of EQUAL square cells on actual transparent alpha, clean uniform spacing with generous transparent gutters, 12% interior padding, no shadows, no marks, no labels, no borders. Every cell has exactly one isolated full-body Luna. Same head size and features across all 16. Chronological row 1: four walking frames, alternating foot contact and opposite leg passing, gentle opposing arm swings and trailing ponytails. Row 2: idle alert, idle blinking with eyes closed, greeting hand raised, greeting hand waving outward. Row 3: anticipation knees bent, takeoff stretch, apex knees tucked, landing crouch. Row 4: sit upright on the floor with legs folded, sit blinking, sleeping in same seated position eyes shut head leaning to shoulder, sleeping second breathing frame. Sitting and sleeping bodies are naturally lower, keep consistent head size. Align each full body centrally within its own cell and align soles or seat contact to consistent baseline in its cell. No scenery, shadows, effects, text, extra props, letters or numbers.

## Ходьба

Use case: stylized-concept. Input image is character identity and precise pixel art reference. Recreate the SAME Luna gothic silver-lilac elf, same pixels, charcoal sweater with butterfly, ring choker, black bows, green eyes and black boots. Production sprite sheet exclusively of a right-facing animated WALK CYCLE, in 4 equally spaced columns by 2 equally spaced rows, 8 isolated characters, actual transparent alpha. Entire body visible, a generous 14% clear padding inside each equally sized cell. Row 1 shows close leg heel contact in front, weight on close leg, far foot lifting visibly, far foot swinging in front. Row 2 shows FAR leg heel contact in front and close leg visibly trailing, weight on far leg, close foot lifting visibly, close foot swinging in front. IMPORTANT: close and far legs must swap roles between rows. Tint the far leg darker so the alternating gait reads. Legs have natural anatomy. Torso direction remains three-quarters right in all cells, shoulders swing minimally, ponytails follow movement. Head stays the same size and relative height. Boots share a stable floor line. Narrow color palette, beautifully readable game pixel clusters at 96px display height, crisp warm outline. Clean transparent pixels around the silhouette. No backdrop, no colored fringe, no floor or contact shadows, no extras, no text.
